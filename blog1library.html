TUCSON GUIDE · LOCAL LOVE

Find out what your public library has to offer!

By Love, Tucson · September 2026

Tucson's public libraries have so much to offer. From books to free admission to your favorite museums!

The Pima County Public Library is a place to read, learn, connect, explore, and find resources that can make everyday life a little easier.

It's never been easier to get a free library card as well! This can be done in-person; online; and via email. In-Person: Visit any of the 27 libraries with a Photo ID and bring a piece of mail or legal document with your name and current address. There are no forms to fill out. Online: Follow the link below to apply for an eCard. Email: Email the library to have your physical card arrive through mail.

Apply for an eCard!

A LITTLE LOCAL REMINDER

Your library card can open a lot of doors.

Pima County Public Library offers access to physical materials, digital resources, programs, learning tools, and more.

So... what can you actually do there?

Here are just a few things worth exploring through the Pima County Public Library that are outside of borrowing books and media.

📚

Culture Passes

A Culture Pass is free admission for two people at participating arts and cultural institutions. This is a great opportunity for you and a buddy to check out a new place for free!

Explore culture passes →
🎧

Read & listen online

Your library also provides digital resources including eBooks, audiobooks, magazines, music, and streaming video. You can also use Libby, a free app that lets you borrow ebooks, digital audiobooks, and magazines from your public library.

Explore the E-Library → Explore Libby →
💻

Learn something new

Find online learning resources, computer help, research tools, homework help, and more.

📅

Attend an event

Libraries around Pima County host programs, classes, activities, and events for people of all ages.
